Grade 4 lesson games

Ten Grade 4 maths games, one per objective, built from the same Fikr lesson-game template as Grades 1–3: Rawi the guide, Ghubar the mischief-maker, guided Learn slides, CUBES think-first on every word problem, a three-step hint ladder and three CRA levels — now on multi-digit multiplication by area model, division with partial quotients, hidden-question two-step problems, factors and primes, comparing fractions, like-denominator fraction sums, decimals to hundredths, angle measurement, place value to the millions, and quadrilateral classification by property.

Every game also renders the نبع/موازي lesson frame under the play cards: the unit's Quranic verse anchor and three-letter roots, the anti-overreach caution, five objectives, the three levels as stations, an exit card of four items with a success criterion, two screen-free homework tasks, a reflection, and the P2 status line.

Prototypes for review — not curriculum-approved and not classroom-tested (P2). Objective mapping, the source ledger and the evidence boundary are recorded in docs/mowazi/g4-expansion.md; the methodology audit against the curriculum folder is in methodology-audit-g1-g4.md.

Each game is one self-contained offline HTML file in this folder. No network, no external fonts, no images — artwork is inline SVG drawn in the page.
