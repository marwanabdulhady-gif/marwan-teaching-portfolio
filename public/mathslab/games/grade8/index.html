Grade 8 lesson games

Seven Grade 8 maths games, one per real lesson document in the Grade 8 mathematics folder (الرياضيات — السنة الثامنة / الصف الثاني الإعدادي / الترم الأول والثاني) — not one per invented objective. Each keeps its document's own bundle code: G08-U01-MAT-L01, G08-U01-MAT-L02, G08-U01-MATH-L02, G08-U01-L03, G08-U02-MAT-L01, G08-U02-MAT-L02 and G08-U02-MATH-L02. Subjects: slope, intercept and graphing a line, Pythagoras in building and the test for a square corner, solving equations and systems, choosing between two linear plans, rate of change from a table and y = mx + b, distance between two plotted points, and the combined functions / Pythagoras lesson with its bound on the model.

Every game also renders the نبع/موازي lesson frame under the play cards: the unit's Quranic verse anchor and three-letter roots, the anti-overreach caution, five objectives, the levels as stations, an exit card of four items with a success criterion, two screen-free homework tasks (30 minutes بلا شاشات, as the documents require alongside the 45-minute lesson), a reflection, and the P2 status line.

Prototypes for review — not curriculum-approved and not classroom-tested (P2). The Grade 8 folder has two overlapping pairs, and they are deliberately not merged. G08-U01-MAT-L01 (points and a slope triangle on a grid) and G08-U02-MAT-L01 (rate of change read out of a table) are both 8.EE.B.5–6 but start from opposite ends of the idea; G08-U01-MAT-L02 (which side is the hypotenuse, the 3-4-5 rope, the converse) and G08-U02-MAT-L02 (the distance between two plotted points) are both 8.G.B.6–8 but work in different settings. G08-U02-MATH-L02 is the combined lesson and adds the one thing the others do not — an explicit limit on the model — so it gets its own game. G08-U01-L03 is a different document again (a 28 kB decision pack that says of itself that it needs development and that its digital build is deferred); its game is the prototype it asks for and carries that caveat. Source mapping and the split decisions are recorded in docs/mowazi/g8-expansion.md.

Each game is one self-contained offline HTML file in this folder. No network, no external fonts, no images — artwork is inline SVG drawn in the page.
