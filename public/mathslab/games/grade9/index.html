Grade 9 lesson games

Seven Grade 9 maths games, one per real lesson document in the Grade 9 mathematics folder (الرياضيات — السنة التاسعة / الصف الثالث الإعدادي / الترم الأول والثاني) — not one per invented objective. Each keeps its document's own bundle code: G09-U01-MAT-L01, G09-U01-MAT-L02, G09-U01-MATH-L02, G09-U01-L03, G09-U02-MAT-L01, G09-U02-MAT-L02 and G09-U02-MATH-L02.

The set is almost entirely quadratics, so the seven games divide the topic rather than repeating it: the curve and its crossings (Parabola Launch), systems and the solution region of inequalities (Region Planner), factoring into roots (Factor Bench), the values table and vector components (Arc Table), completing the square with the general formula and the discriminant (Complete the Square), maximum versus minimum (Vertex Extremes), and intervals of increase and decrease with the limits of a claim (Quadratic Apps).

Every game also renders the نبع/موازي lesson frame under the play cards: the unit's Quranic verse anchor and three-letter roots, the anti-overreach caution, five objectives, the levels as stations, an exit card of four items with a success criterion, two screen-free homework tasks (the documents' own 30 minutes بلا شاشات beside the 45-minute lesson), a reflection, and the P2 status line.

Prototypes for review — not curriculum-approved and not classroom-tested (P2). Two things in this grade need a human decision. First, G09-U01-MATH-L02 (factoring polynomials) and G09-U02-MAT-L01 (completing the square and the general formula) are different lessons that finish the same equations; they are kept as two games, one owning the factoring route and one owning the completing-the-square route, and neither works the other's method. Second, G09-U01-L03 (quadratic function and vectors) says of itself that it is an unapproved draft whose HTML and digital game build are deferred, and that its numbers are hypothetical and prove no real aircraft performance; its game is the prototype the document asks for, and the caveat travels with it. Source mapping and every split decision are recorded in docs/mowazi/g9-expansion.md.

Each game is one self-contained offline HTML file in this folder. No network, no external fonts, no images — artwork is inline SVG drawn in the page.
