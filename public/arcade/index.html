The points ladder

Every correct answer is worth 10 points, a streak of five adds +5, and finishing a full session pays a completion bonus. Nothing is ever taken away.

How a math block works here

Set up in under 60 seconds. Nothing to install, nothing to project from a teacher laptop account.

  1. 1

    Pick a level

    Kindergarten counting through grade-5 fractions — or let the trainer choose based on the saved points.

  2. 2

    Name the teams

    Split the room in half, type two team names, and the rope appears on the board. One student at a time, everyone solving.

  3. 3

    Solve & pull

    Every correct answer tugs the rope one notch. Wrong answers cost time, not points — nobody shrinks.

  4. 4

    Earn & climb

    points lands on the shared progress board, streaks grow, and the class leaderboard resets on Friday.

Why teachers keep the tab open all year

Big screen ready

Chunky type and rope animations that still read from the back row of a 32-foot room. Hit projector mode and the whole interface scales up.

100% free, no signup

No accounts, no ads, no uploads. School-filter friendly: open the link and play. Everything lives in this browser only.

Curriculum aligned

Counting, comparing, skip counting, place value, facts, fractions, area, time and money — mapped to grade bands K–5.

Focus-friendly

A calm mode strips timers, motion and sound for students with ADHD, anxiety or sensory sensitivity. Same math, fewer surprises.

Printable pack

Generate a fresh worksheet for any band, print it, and let the pencil-and-paper crowd warm up before the screen round.

Works on the bad Wi-Fi day

Static, self-contained, no API calls. Once loaded, it keeps working with the network completely off.
