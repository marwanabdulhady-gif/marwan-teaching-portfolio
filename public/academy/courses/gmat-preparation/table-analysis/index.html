Read the table strategically

Identify the unit and the comparison asked for, sort the relevant column, then test each statement independently. Absolute profit and profit margin are different quantities.

Identify

Read the column headings and units.

Reorganize

Sort the relevant column; avoid scanning blindly.

Verify

Calculate only the values needed to judge the statement.

Worked example

A division has revenue of $200,000 and costs of $150,000. Find its operating profit and profit margin.

Profit = $50,000. Profit margin = $50,000 / $200,000 = 25%.
