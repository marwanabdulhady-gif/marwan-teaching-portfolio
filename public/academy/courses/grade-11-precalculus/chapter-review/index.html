1.3 · Algebraic Expressions & Factoring

Read the structure before doing algebra: exponent rules → operations → special products → factoring. For factoring, use the classroom routine rather than guessing.

Mr. Marwan’s factoring system
  1. Check the GCF first.
  2. Count the terms.
  3. Choose the matching pattern.
  4. Factor.
  5. Ask: “Can I factor again?”

1.4 · Rational Expressions

Restrictions come from the original denominator. Factor before cancelling, and cancel factors — never terms joined by addition or subtraction.

Operations routine: Multiply → factor and cancel. Divide → Keep–Change–Flip, then factor. Add/Subtract → factor denominators, build the LCD, rewrite, combine, simplify.
Mr. Marwan’s preferred compound-fraction method: find one LCD for all small denominators and multiply the entire numerator and entire denominator by it.
a−m=1ama^{-m}=\frac1{a^m}a−m=am1​

For a two-term radical numerator or denominator, use its conjugate when rationalizing.

1.8 · Inequalities

For linear inequalities, remember that multiplying or dividing by a negative reverses the sign. For nonlinear and rational inequalities, use critical numbers and a sign chart.

Mr. Marwan’s sign-chart routine
  1. Move everything to one side and compare with 0.
  2. Factor completely.
  3. Find critical numbers.
  4. Split the number line into intervals.
  5. Determine signs and choose the required intervals.
  6. Check endpoints.

Rational inequalities: ZN may be included when equality is allowed; ZD is never included. Do not cross-multiply by an expression of unknown sign.

Mr. Marwan’s circle routine
  1. Make the coefficients of x2x^2x2 and y2y^2y2 equal to 1.
  2. Move the constant.
  3. Group x-terms and y-terms.
  4. Complete both squares.
  5. Compare with standard form.

For diameter problems, midpoint first. For a circle tangent to an axis, radius equals the perpendicular distance from the center to that axis.
