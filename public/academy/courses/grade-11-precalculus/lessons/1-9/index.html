General Form → Standard Form

⌄
Explanation
x2+y2+Dx+Ey+F=0x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey+F=0x2+y2+Dx+Ey+F=0
  1. Make squared coefficients 1.
  2. Move the constant.
  3. Group x-terms and y-terms.
  4. Complete both squares.
  5. Compare with standard form.

Why are we completing the square?

We want each x-group and y-group to become a perfect-square binomial so the center and radius become visible.

The pattern

u2+bu+(b2)2=(u+b2)2u^2+bu+\left(\frac{b}{2}\right)^2=\left(u+\frac{b}{2}\right)^2u2+bu+(2b​)2=(u+2b​)2

Take half of the linear coefficient, then square it.

Step 1 · Start and move the constant

x2+y2−4x+10y+13=0x^2+y^2-4x+10y+13=0x2+y2−4x+10y+13=0
(x2−4x)+(y2+10y)=−13(x^2-4x)+(y^2+10y)=-13(x2−4x)+(y2+10y)=−13

Step 2 · Build the x perfect square

Show the calculation in order, then write the perfect-square binomial.

Common mistake

Common mistakes

  • Distance: keep subtraction order consistent.
  • Midpoint: average corresponding coordinates.
  • Circle center: reverse the signs inside parentheses.
  • Standard form uses r2r^2r2, not rrr.
  • Diameter: midpoint first.
  • General form: normalize squared coefficients before completing squares.
  • Completing squares: add the same values to both sides.
  • Tangency: radius is perpendicular distance to the axis.
