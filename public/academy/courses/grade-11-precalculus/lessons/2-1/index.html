What is the domain?

When an even root is the entire denominator: radicand > 0. For sums or differences containing a root, require a real root and separately exclude zeros of the whole denominator.

Piecewise

✕ Pick the first rule in the list.

Difference quotient

✕ Cancel before factoring.

Fraction domain

✕ Exclude numerator zeros.

✓ Exclude denominator zeros.

Even root as the denominator

✕ Use radicand ≥ 0.

✓ Use radicand > 0 because the denominator cannot be zero.
