1. Rules for Inequalities

⌄

Addition and subtraction work as they do in equations. The important change happens when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.

A≤B⇒A+C≤B+CA\le B\Rightarrow A+C\le B+CA≤B⇒A+C≤B+C
C>0:A≤B⇒AC≤BCC>0:\quad A\le B\Rightarrow AC\le BCC>0:A≤B⇒AC≤BC
C<0:A≤B⇒AC≥BCC<0:\quad A\le B\Rightarrow AC\ge BCC<0:A≤B⇒AC≥BC
Common mistakeKey Rule: multiplying or dividing both sides by a negative number reverses the inequality sign.
Worked example

Mini example

4. Rational Inequalities

⌄
ExplanationZN · Zero of numerator

May be included when equality is allowed.

Common mistakeZD · Zero of denominator

Never included because the expression is undefined there.

Common mistakeImportant: do not cross-multiply by an expression containing xxx unless its sign is known.
