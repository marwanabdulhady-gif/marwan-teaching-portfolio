Chapter 2 Review · Differentiation

A full exam-preparation review built around Mr. Marwan’s quiz and test style: definitions, mixed differentiation, differentiability, Chain Rule, implicit differentiation, and related rates.

Review roadmap

⌄
Explanation

This is an exam-preparation rebuild, not a formula sheet. Read the full question, identify what is being tested, choose the rule or model, show the required work, and simplify only after the derivative structure is correct.

2.1 · Build the derivative

Tangent slope, instantaneous rate, and derivative from the limit definition.

2.2 + 2.3 · Differentiate fluently

Power, sum/difference, trig, Product, Quotient, higher derivatives, velocity and acceleration.

Extension · Differentiability

Continuity first, then compare left- and right-hand derivatives at the breaking point.

2.4 · Chain Rule

See the layers: outside → inside. Expect mixed Product/Quotient + Chain questions.

2.5 · Implicit

Differentiate every term with respect to x; every y-term needs its derivative factor.

2.6 · Related Rates

Translate words into signed rates, build the model, differentiate in time, then substitute the instant.

Part C · Piecewise functions & differentiability

⌄
Key takeaway

Mr. Marwan’s breaking-point routine

1. Identify the breaking point x=cx=cx=c.

2. Check continuity first.

3. Differentiate each piece.

4. Use the correct side and compare f−′(c)f'_{-}(c)f−′​(c) with f+′(c)f'_{+}(c)f+′​(c).

5. State the conclusion.

f differentiable at c⟹f continuous at cf\text{ differentiable at }c\Longrightarrow f\text{ continuous at }cf differentiable at c⟹f continuous at c
Common mistake. Continuity alone does not guarantee differentiability. Corners, cusps, and vertical tangents still need attention.

Part D · Chain Rule & exam-style combinations

⌄
Key takeaway

Mr. Marwan’s inside / outside method

See the layers before differentiating. Differentiate the outside while keeping the inside unchanged, then multiply by the derivative of the inside.

dydx=dydu⋅dudx\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy}{du}\cdot\frac{du}{dx}dxdy​=dudy​⋅dxdu​
Quiz-style composition

Question: If f(x)=(2x+1)3f(x)=(2x+1)^3f(x)=(2x+1)3 and g(x)=cos⁡(πx)g(x)=\cos(\pi x)g(x)=cos(πx), find (f∘g)′(x)(f\circ g)'(x)(f∘g)′(x).

Method 1: use f′(g(x))g′(x)f'(g(x))g'(x)f′(g(x))g′(x). Method 2: compose first, then differentiate. You should be comfortable with both.

Part E · Implicit Differentiation

⌄
Key takeaway

Routine

Differentiate both sides with respect to xxx. A term containing yyy needs Chain Rule. If xyxyxy appears, use Product Rule. Then collect every dy/dxdy/dxdy/dx term, factor it, and solve.

Core pattern

Question: Find dy/dxdy/dxdy/dx if x2+y2=25x^2+y^2=25x2+y2=25.

2x+2ydydx=02x+2y\frac{dy}{dx}=02x+2ydxdy​=0dydx=−xy(y≠0)\boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{x}{y}}\qquad(y\ne0)dxdy​=−yx​​(y=0)
Common mistake. ddx(yn)\frac d{dx}(y^n)dxd​(yn) is not just nyn−1ny^{n-1}nyn−1; multiply by dy/dxdy/dxdy/dx.

Part F · Related Rates

⌄
Key takeaway

Mr. Marwan’s exam routine

1. Read the full problem.

2. Draw/understand the situation.

3. Identify changing quantities.

4. Translate words into signed rates.

5. Mark known and wanted rates.

6. Write the geometric/physical model.

7. Reduce variables when needed.

8. Differentiate with respect to ttt.

9. Substitute the instant and solve.

10. State direction/sign and units.

Fast recognition

Question: A circular ripple expands at dr/dt=1 ft/sdr/dt=1\,\text{ft/s}dr/dt=1ft/s. How fast is its area changing when r=4 ftr=4\,\text{ft}r=4ft?

A=πr2⇒dAdt=2πrdrdt=8π ft2/sA=\pi r^2\Rightarrow\frac{dA}{dt}=2\pi r\frac{dr}{dt}=8\pi\,\text{ft}^2/\text{s}A=πr2⇒dtdA​=2πrdtdr​=8πft2/s
Common mistake. Do not substitute changing lengths before differentiating unless you have first reduced the model correctly. Keep variables changing until the rate equation exists.
