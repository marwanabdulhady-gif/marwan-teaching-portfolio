Implicit Differentiation · Follow every y

Differentiate the relation without solving for yyy first. The Chain Rule from Lesson 2.4 is active whenever y=y(x)y=y(x)y=y(x), so every differentiated y-function must carry its derivative.

Use Mr. Marwan’s routine to differentiate implicit relations, collect dy/dx terms, and find slopes at points.

Explicit or implicit?

⌄

In an explicit equation, yyy is already isolated. In an implicit equation, xxx and yyy can appear together. We can differentiate the relation directly.

Explanation
Explicit
y=x2+3xy=x^2+3xy=x2+3x

Differentiate the right side normally.

Explanation
Implicit
x2+y2=25x^2+y^2=25x2+y2=25

Differentiate both sides with respect to xxx.

Key takeaway
First decision

Do not waste time solving for yyy unless the problem specifically asks you to. Implicit differentiation is designed to work with the relation as written.

The key idea: y depends on x

Mr. Marwan’s implicit differentiation routine

⌄
STEP 1
Differentiate both sides
Work with respect to xxx.
STEP 2
Track every y-term
Attach dydx\frac{dy}{dx}dxdy​ whenever a function of yyy is differentiated.
STEP 3
Collect derivative terms
Move all terms containing dydx\frac{dy}{dx}dxdy​ to one side.
STEP 4
Factor
Take dydx\frac{dy}{dx}dxdy​ as a common factor.
STEP 5
Isolate
Divide to solve for dydx\frac{dy}{dx}dxdy​.
STEP 6
Then substitute
If a point is given, substitute only after the derivative formula is ready.
