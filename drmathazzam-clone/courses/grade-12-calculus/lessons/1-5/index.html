Vertical Asymptotes

Definition

Vertical asymptote

The line x=cx=cx=c is a vertical asymptote if the function approaches +∞+\infty+∞ or −∞-\infty−∞ as xxx approaches ccc from at least one side.
lim⁡x→c−f(x)=±∞orlim⁡x→c+f(x)=±∞⇒x=c is a VA\lim_{x\to c^-}f(x)=\pm\infty\quad\text{or}\quad\lim_{x\to c^+}f(x)=\pm\infty\Rightarrow x=c\text{ is a VA}x→c−lim​f(x)=±∞orx→c+lim​f(x)=±∞⇒x=c is a VA
