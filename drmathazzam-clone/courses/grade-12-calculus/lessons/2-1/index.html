The Derivative & the Tangent Line Problem

Start with a familiar slope, let a secant line approach a tangent line, build the derivative from a limit, and use that same idea to measure instantaneous motion.

Secant Line → Tangent Line

⌄
Definition

Secant line

A secant line passes through two points on a curve. Its slope is an average rate of change.
Explanation

Keep one point fixed and move the second point toward it. Then Δx→0\Delta x\to0Δx→0. The secant slopes approach one limiting value: the slope of the tangent line.

Two times are used, so this is a secant slope.
